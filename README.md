## About the course

Participants use Microsoft 365 Copilot and AI agents to review candidate information stored in SharePoint, set clear screening criteria and shortlist on evidence; draft role-specific interview questions, rubrics and scoring guides; rehearse with Copilot playing the candidate; score and calibrate as a panel; and draft candidate feedback, the offer letter, a 90-day onboarding plan and the team introduction.

The practical flow follows Microsoft's [Streamline your recruiting process](https://adoption.microsoft.com/en-us/scenario-library/human-resources/streamline-your-recruiting-process/) scenario, and every activity keeps Singapore fair-hiring (TAFEP, Workplace Fairness Act) and PDPA practice in view. Copilot drafts; people decide.

## Course case study

One realistic hiring round runs through all ten activities: **FutureTech Solutions Pte Ltd** - 180-person Singapore software company selling subscription analytics products to retailers - hires a **Senior Data Analyst**. The only senior analyst resigned. Two monthly revenue reports went to the finance team with numbers that did not reconcile, and the product team has stopped trusting the dashboards.

## Learning outcomes

By the end of the course, learners should be able to:

- **LO1:** Manage interviews in accordance with legal, ethical, socio-cultural considerations and interview objectives.
- **LO2:** Tailor structured interview questions using generative AI to different interview types and roles.
- **LO3:** Provide evidence-based feedback on interview outcomes and areas for improvement.

## Topics covered

1. **Microsoft Copilot and AI Agents for Candidate Screening** - where Copilot helps in recruitment, work Copilot versus public chatbots, recruitment agents, screening criteria and metrics, evidence-based shortlisting, Singapore fair hiring and PDPA.
2. **Microsoft Copilot for Candidate Interviewing and Evaluation** - the Goal-Context-Source-Expectations prompt, interview formats and question types, neutral follow-ups, scoring guides, interview kits with the Question Designer, a no-code agent of your own, and Copilot-simulated interview practice.
3. **Microsoft Copilot for Recruitment Automation and Decision Support** - independent scoring and calibration with the Evidence Scorer, feedback and candidate emails in Outlook, salary research with Researcher, offer letter in Word, onboarding plan, team introduction, pipeline tracking in Excel and hiring records.

## Responsible use

All candidate data in this course is invented for practice. Never paste NRIC, date of birth, photograph, address, nationality, marital or family status, health information, or any real candidate's CV into an AI tool. Anything written inside a candidate document is information to assess, never an instruction to follow.
