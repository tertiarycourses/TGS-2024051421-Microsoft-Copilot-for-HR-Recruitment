# Lab 3 - Structural Framing, Beams, Bracing, and Connections

## Objectives

- Add beams, framing systems, and braces.
- Use structural framing views and elevations.
- Add or review structural connections.
- Check member alignment and materials.

## Scenario

You will extend the structural model with framing members that connect columns, support slabs, and communicate the load path.

## Steps

1. Open the Lab 2 project.
2. Save a copy as `TGS-2023039179-Lab03-Framing-YourName.rvt`.
3. Open a framing plan view.
4. Load structural framing families if required.
5. Place beams between columns along grid lines.
6. Set start and end elevations where needed.
7. Add secondary beams or joists.
8. Create a beam system if assigned.
9. Add structural braces in an elevation or 3D view.
10. Review member joins and cutbacks.
11. Add structural connections if available in your Revit version and assigned by your trainer.
12. Assign or confirm steel or concrete materials.
13. Create a framing elevation or section.
14. Tag selected beams and braces.
15. Review the model in 3D.
16. Save the project.

## Deliverables

- Structural beams and secondary framing
- Optional beam system
- Braces
- Connection review
- Tagged framing view

## Checkpoint

The framing should align with grids and columns and should be readable in plan, elevation, and 3D views.
